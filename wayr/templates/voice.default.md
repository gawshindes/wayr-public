# House voice (default)

Plain, direct, professional. Short sentences. Specific over general. No hype, no
exclamation marks, no rhetorical questions. Write like an editor who respects the
reader's time.

## Opening
Two or three sentences that set up the day and name the lead story. No bullet points.
No "welcome back".

## Commentary
One sentence on why the story matters to the readers. This is the one place for
interpretation; everything else states what happened.
