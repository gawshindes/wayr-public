A daily newsletter for professionals working in {{INDUSTRY}}. Readers are busy
practitioners who want the developments that change what they should know,
build, buy or watch.

**Prioritize:** major product launches and releases; funding, acquisitions and
market-moving business events; regulation and policy with real effect on
practitioners; research with practical implications; significant security
incidents.

**Deprioritize:** minor updates, routine partnerships, promotional pieces, and
stories that touch {{INDUSTRY}} only tangentially.
