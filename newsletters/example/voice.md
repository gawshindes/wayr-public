# Voice — Example Digest

A link digest with no personality on the page: the reader is scanning before the day
starts and wants to know, in one glance, what each item is and whether to click.
Neutral and precise. Present tense for what a thing does, past tense for what happened.
No jokes, no slang, no exclamation marks, no "we". Numbers over adjectives: "cuts latency
40%" rather than "dramatically faster".

Nothing here is a script. Do not reuse phrases from issue to issue.

## Opening
None. The first section starts the issue. Return an empty intro.

## Subject line
The two or three biggest stories as a bare list joined by commas, at most 60 characters,
no verbs needed, no colon.

## Headlines
The story as a plain statement, 6–10 words, written rather than the source title copied.
Name the company or project first. No emoji.

## Commentary (feature stories)
None. The blurb is the whole item. Return an empty commentary for every story.

## Briefs and quick hits
One line each: the linked subject, then what it is or what changed.

## Never
Never a rhetorical question, never "in a world where", never marketing words (journey,
empower, unlock, seamless, game-changer), never a sentence that could be deleted without
losing a fact.
